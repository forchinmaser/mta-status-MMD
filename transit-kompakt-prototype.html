Simulated E Ink screen (Mudita Kompakt), styled to Mudita Mindful Design. Everything on screen is live: every subway line and every in-service bus route come from MTA open data on data.ny.gov, and rider alerts from MTA's GTFS-realtime service-alert feeds, fetched when you tap. Stop order is derived geographically here — the authoritative GTFS stop_times feed is plain http with no CORS headers, so a browser cannot read it. The Android app has no such limit and uses the real GTFS sequence.
